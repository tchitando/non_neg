 Looking at simclr.py:178-184, NCL uses the standard NT-Xent (InfoNCE) contrastive loss (simclr_loss_func), logged as train_nce_loss. The only NCL-specific
  modification is that projector outputs z are passed through rep_relu (reparameterized ReLU) before computing the loss — so the loss is the same InfoNCE
  objective, just applied to non-negative features.

  There's no separate validation loss tracked — the training signal is entirely the NCE loss on augmented pairs. The validation metric during training is the
  online linear classifier accuracy (val_acc), not a loss value.
